Our Philosophy

Grown with love, delivered with purpose

Satvik Fruits was born from a single belief: that eating a fruit should feel like biting into nature itself — no wax coating, no ripening chambers, no cold-store aftertaste.

We cultivate over 15 acres of mixed-fruit orchards in the Konkan belt and the Deccan plateau, using traditional Indian farming wisdom alongside modern organic practices.

Farm to Doorstep

From Orchard to Your Table

Every order triggers a fresh pick. No warehouses, no delays.

🌱
Step 01

Organic Cultivation

We use compost, neem-cake, and traditional intercropping. Zero synthetic pesticides — ever.

🤲
Step 02

Hand-picked at Peak

Our farmers hand-select each fruit at the precise moment of ripeness, based on colour, aroma, and touch.

📦
Step 03

Gentle Packing

Fruits are cushioned in eco-friendly banana-leaf lining and corrugated boxes to survive transit without bruising.

Step 04

24-Hour Delivery

Express logistics to your doorstep within 24 hours of picking. Track live from your phone.

Why Satvik

The difference you can taste and trust

Most fruit sold in India travels 5–7 days in cold storage and is treated with ethylene gas to ripen artificially. We never do that.

Satvik means "pure" in Sanskrit. It's not just our name — it's the standard we hold every fruit to before it leaves our farm.
